# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers are responsible for designing user interfaces and experiences that meet both user needs and business goals. They collaborate closely with Product Managers to define wireframes, prototypes, and design systems, and work alongside Developers to ensure faithful implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes
- Conduct usability testing and synthesize user feedback
- Maintain design system consistency across the product
- Define UX acceptance criteria alongside the Product Manager
- Review implemented UI against design specifications

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and visually consistent user experiences
- Reduce rework by catching UX issues before development begins
- Ensure user needs are represented in every product decision

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and handoff sessions with Developers
- Discovery workshops and feature alignment sessions with Product Managers
- Sprint ceremonies and retrospectives

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with **Product Managers** on feature discovery and requirements definition
- Provides design specs and assets to **Developers** for implementation
- Participates in sprint planning and retrospectives alongside the full team

---

## QA / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers ensure that delivered software meets quality standards through systematic testing strategies. They bridge the gap between development and production by identifying defects early and validating acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans, test cases, and test suites
- Perform regression, integration, and exploratory testing
- Track, report, and triage defects
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria definition with the Product Manager
- Champion shift-left testing practices within the team

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Increase confidence in every release through comprehensive coverage
- Reduce cost of quality by catching issues early

### Typical Communication
- Test reports and defect logs shared with Developers and Project Managers
- Acceptance criteria review sessions with Product Managers
- Quality metrics and risk summaries in sprint reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with **Developers** on testability, coverage, and defect resolution
- Validates acceptance criteria with **Product Managers** before and after delivery
- Reports quality risks and metrics to **Project Managers** for risk tracking

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects provide technical direction and ensure architectural decisions are sound, scalable, and aligned with the project's long-term goals. They act as a bridge between engineering execution and strategic planning.

### Responsibilities
- Define and document technical architecture and standards
- Lead technical design reviews and Architecture Decision Record (ADR) creation
- Guide technology choices and enforce engineering best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical debt proactively
- Mentor Developers on implementation approach and quality

### Goals
- Ensure the system is scalable, maintainable, and secure
- Reduce long-term technical risk through sound architectural decisions
- Align technical execution with product and business strategy

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design documents and ADRs
- Technical feasibility assessments shared with Product Managers
- Code reviews, tech talks, and mentoring sessions with Developers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Guides **Developers** on implementation approach and best practices
- Reports technical risks and architectural constraints to **Project Managers**

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Delivery Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies and remove impediments to ensure the team can operate at a sustainable pace. They coach the team on agile principles and continuously improve team dynamics and delivery flow.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify, track, and remove blockers and impediments
- Monitor team velocity and delivery metrics
- Coach team members on agile and lean practices
- Shield the team from external interruptions and scope creep

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver predictably and sustainably
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Maximize team focus and minimize waste

### Typical Communication
- Impediment logs and team health reports shared with Project Managers
- Sprint readiness and backlog refinement sessions with Product Managers
- Daily facilitation and retrospective facilitation with all team members

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports **Project Managers** with delivery tracking and risk escalation
- Coaches **Developers** on agile practices, process hygiene, and self-organization
- Coordinates with **Product Managers** on sprint readiness and backlog health

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders / Executive Sponsors provide strategic sponsorship, funding, and organizational alignment for the project. They are accountable for the project's business outcomes and serve as the ultimate decision-making authority for scope and priority at the executive level.

### Responsibilities
- Champion the project at the organizational level
- Approve major scope, budget, and timeline changes
- Remove organizational blockers that are beyond the delivery team's authority
- Review and act on escalated risks and issues
- Participate in milestone and governance reviews

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain organizational support and funding throughout the project lifecycle
- Align project outcomes with strategic business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Executive status reports and dashboards from Project Managers
- Strategic priority alignment sessions with Product Managers
- Milestone reviews and governance gate presentations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receives status reports and risk escalations from **Project Managers**
- Aligns with **Product Managers** on strategic priorities and roadmap direction
- Participates in milestone and governance reviews across the entire team

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded set of 8 personas covers the full project delivery lifecycle — from strategic sponsorship and architecture through design, development, quality, and continuous improvement.
- **New persona prompt examples:**
  - Use the **UX/UI Designer** persona to explore design handoff workflows and usability testing scenarios.
  - Use the **QA / Test Engineer** persona to frame shift-left testing strategies, defect triage, and release readiness conversations.
  - Use the **Technical Lead / Architect** persona to discuss architectural trade-offs, technical debt, and feasibility assessments.
  - Use the **Scrum Master / Delivery Coach** persona to model agile ceremony facilitation, impediment removal, and team coaching.
  - Use the **Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor** persona to explore escalation paths, governance reviews, and strategic alignment exercises.
